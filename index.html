JoSAA / CSAB Strategy

Fill choices strictly in order of personal preference regardless of cutoffs. Always rank top branch at preferred NIT/IIIT above lower branches at average NITs.

2

Home State Advantage

50% seats in NITs and State Universities are reserved for state candidates. Cutoffs are significantly relaxed for Home State candidates in rounds 5-6.

3

ROI vs Campus Brand
